# Restaurant BJ — Website Game Plan

**Status:** Brainstorm complete. Waiting on photos → then build.
**Location:** Vila Olímpica, Barcelona (near UPF Ciutadella, school in front)
**Customers:** UPF students & teachers, school staff/families, government & construction workers, occasional tourists.

## Positioning

Family-run for years. Cheap, fast, honest food. That's the identity — no pretension. Customers want to know *what's for lunch today* before walking over.

## The Site: one page, two languages (ES / EN)

1. **Today's menu** — top of page, big. Simple text block, retyped every 2–3 days when it changes. Includes bocadillo + coffee deal of the day.
2. **Menú del día price + opening hours** — visible without scrolling.
3. **Fixed carta** — set once, rarely touched.
4. **Footer** — WhatsApp button, Google Maps embed, one line of family story.

**Not building:** reservations, online ordering, blog, anything needing daily maintenance.

## Update Workflow (the most important design constraint)

Editing today's menu must take **under 2 minutes on a phone**. The whole build is designed around this.
